Out-of-Network Information

Out-of-Network insurance accepted:

Tricare*
    *Tricare Prime requires that a yearly deductible be met.

We are happy to see patients out-of-network; however, full payment is expected at time of service. 

We will provide you with the necessary receipts needed to file with your insurance company. 

Reimbursement rates vary and are provided by your individual plan.
