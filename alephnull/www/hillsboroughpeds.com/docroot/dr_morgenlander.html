Our Staff

Dr. Marci Morgenlander is a 1988 graduate of the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine. She completed her pediatric residency training at Duke University in 1991 and completed one year of subspecialty training in pediatric pulmonary medicine at Duke University in 1992. Dr. Morgenlander has been certified by the American Board of Pediatrics since 1991. She is currently pursuing a Master’s in Public Health at the Gillings School of Global Public Health at the University of North Carolina.

Over the past two decades, she has worked as a pediatric urgent care physician for Kaiser Permanente, Carolina Permanente, and most recently for Accent Urgent Care in Raleigh and Cary. Her pediatric interests include respiratory illnesses, preventive medicine, the medical home model, and family oriented care.

Dr. Morgenlander and her husband enjoy attending the multitude of sporting, musical and theatre events of their four children. In addition, she is an active volunteer at CE Jordan High School and St. Thomas More Church. She also enjoys reading, walking, theatre, Duke Basketball and tap dance.
