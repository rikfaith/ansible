ANNOUNCEMENT

January 6, 2017

Dear Families of Hillsborough Pediatrics,

I am delighted to announce that Hillsborough Pediatric and Adolescent Medicine will become part of Chapel Hill Pediatrics and Adolescents, effective January 14, 2017.  Our practice has always strived to give our patients and families the very highest quality care possible, in the context of personal relationships with our families. The true joy of the practice of medicine is in these relationships.

Chapel Hill Pediatrics shares our commitment to high quality, personalized care and relationships with families, and has been a leader in North Carolina pediatric practice, providing care for Triangle-area children and families for over 50 years.

Your children’s care with our Hillsborough Pediatrics clinicians, Tammy Schaub, CPNP, and myself, Dr Clepper-Faith, will continue in our Hillsborough office. I will be on sabbatical starting April, 2017 and will return to the office on or before July 1st 2017. During this time, Tammy Schaub and the physicians of Chapel Hill Pediatrics will be available to provide your children’s care.

We will now be able to offer you even more services and resources, such as access to pediatric services 365 days a year (including Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays), after-hour evening care, walk in hours, and expanded in-office laboratory services.

I look forward to joining the Chapel Hill Pediatrics family in the care of your children and continuing to serve your family’s needs. Please contact us with any questions.

Sincerely,

Melissa Clepper-Faith MD, FAAP
Hillsborough Pediatric and Adolescent Medicine, PLLC
