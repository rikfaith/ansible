Dr. Suzanne Dvergsten comes to us from Minneapolis, Minnesota after spending 3 years in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania as associate professor at the University of Pittsburgh. She graduated with honors from St. Olaf College and the University of Minnesota Medical School. She completed her pediatric residency at the University of Minnesota and has been in pediatric practice for the last 18 years.

She is board certified in pediatrics and her medical interests include newborn care, growth and development, dermatology, and asthma.

In her spare time, Dr. Dvergsten enjoys spending time with her husband and two sons. She loves swimming, ice skating, and reading mystery novels.
