====== Chili ======

These recipes are all based on Rik's Mom's Chili recipe.

===== Original =====
{{recipes:mom-chili-recipe-cropped.jpg?58x75}}

  * 1/2 pound, ground chuck
  * chopped onion
  * 8oz tomato sauce
  * 1 can tomato soup
  * 15oz red kidney beans
  * 1 tsp flour
  * 2-3 tsp chili powder
  * 1 tsp brown sugar
  * cook 25 minutes


===== Melissa's Variation =====

  * Finely chop an onion
  * Saute onion in olive oil until translucent
  * Add 1 pound ground turkey thigh
  * Cook turkey and brown
  * Pour off any remaining liquid
  * Add 2 15oz cans of kidney beans, drained
  * Add 1 15oz can of diced tomatoes
  * Add 2 tbsp [[herbs | Southwest Seasoning]]
  * Add 2 tbsp flour
  * Add 2 tbsp brown sugar
  * Add 1/8 tsp [[herbs | chipotle]]
  * Stir well
  * Cook slowly for an hour

===== Rik's Variation =====

  * No flour
  * Only 1 tbsp brown sugar or succant

===== Rik's Later Variations =====
  * No flour or sugar
  * Instead of 2 cans of kidney beans, use 1 can kidney beans, 1 can pinto beans, and 1 can black beans

{{recipes:20080309-124618.jpg?200x150}}
===== Vegetarian Variation =====

  * Instead of 1 pound of ground turkey, substitute an additional 15oz can of kidney beans.
