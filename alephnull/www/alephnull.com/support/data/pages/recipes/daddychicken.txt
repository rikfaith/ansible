====== Daddy Chicken ======

  * Preheat oven to 360°F
  * Obtain 4 whole chicken legs, cut in half
  * Place legs and thighs in pan, removing excess skin where possible
  * Drizzle olive oil over chicken
  * Cover first side of chicken with salt, pepper, [[herbs | Fines Herbes]], and basil (resist all temptation to add additional herbs)
  * Cover pan with aluminum foil

It all looks like this before it goes in the oven the first time:

{{recipes:chicken.jpg?193x130| Uncooked chicken}}

  * Bake for 30 minutes
  * Remove from oven
    * Turn chicken over
    * Cover the second side with pepper [[herbs | Fines Herbes]], and basil (**no** salt this time; but a little more olive oil is ok)
    * Replace aluminum foil cover
  * Bake for 30 minutes more
  * Remove aluminum foil cover
  * Bake for an additional 10-15 minutes
