Meatloaf

350 degrees

  * 1 pound ground turkey thigh
  * 1/2 pound ground beef
  * 1 cup tomato or V8 juice
  * 3/4 cup oatmeal, uncooked
  * 1 egg, beaten
  * 1/4 cup chopped onion
  * 1 and 1/2  teaspoons Southwest seasoning, or salt/pepper to taste


  
 1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. combine all ingredients and mix well.

 2. Press firmly into ungreased 8x4x2 inch loaf pan. bake about 1 hour.

 3. Let stand 5 minutes before slicing. Serves 6 to 8.
