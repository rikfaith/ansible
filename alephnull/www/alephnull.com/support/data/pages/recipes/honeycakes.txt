====== Honeycakes (Banini cakes) ======

(From Aleksa, August 2023)


  * 150g (5.2oz) of butter
  * 150g (5.2oz) of sugar 
  * 1 egg
  * 4 spoons of milk
  * 4 spoons of honey
  * 1 teaspoon baking soda
  * cinnamon, per taste
  * flour, if needed

Mix all ingredients and form a batter. Make shapes with cookie cutters. Bake at 350F until done.
