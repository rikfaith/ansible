Arugula salad dressing

  * Fresh lemon juice
  * Olive oil
  * Minced Garlic
  * Finely grated parmesan
  * Fresh pepper
  * Salt

Whisk together lemon, oil, and garlic. Add parmesan and mix. Add salt and pepper to taste and mix. Serve!
Make salad with arugula, sliced cherry tomatoes, and avocado.
