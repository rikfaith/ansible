Magic Cookie Bars

Oven: 350 degrees

  * 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter
  * 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  * 1  (14) oz can sweetened condensed milk
  * 2 cups (12 oz) semi sweet chocolate chips, or butterscotch chips
  * 1 1/3 cups flaked coconut
  * 1 cup chopped nuts


  - Preheat oven to 350 degrees, or 325 degrees for glass dish. In a 13x9 inch baking pan, melt the butter in oven.
  - Sprinkle graham cracker crumbs over butter; pour sweetened condensed milk evenly over crumbs. 
  - Top with remaining ingredients and press down firmly with fork.
  - Bake 25 minutes or until lightly browned. Cool. Cut into bars or diamonds. Store covered at room temperature.
