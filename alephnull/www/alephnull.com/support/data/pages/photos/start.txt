====== Photos ======
=====Strawberry bush=====
Along the trails at [[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medoc_Mountain_State_Park | Medoc Mountain]], we were stuck by the [[wp>Euonymus_americanus]] bush, with orange berries that apparently had burst from a strawberry-like structure.  These evergreen shrubs are in the [[wp>Celastraceae]] family and very different from eatable stawberries (which are in the [[wp>Fragaria]] family).
