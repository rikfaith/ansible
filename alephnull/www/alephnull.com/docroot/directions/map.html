Directions to 4001 Woodland Park Drive

These are sample directions to give you an idea of where we live. It will be best to find a route via google maps
  • From Chapel Hill:
    1. North on NC-86 for about 7 miles past I-40
    2. Right on US-70A (at stop light), for about 4.5 miles (merge onto US-70)
    3. Left on Pleasant Green Rd (at light near muffler shop; before fire station)
    4. See "From US-70 and Pleasant Green", below
  • From CFS:
    1. North on Friends School Rd
    2. Right on Murphy School Rd
    3. Left on Mt. Herman Church Rd (at blinking caution light)
    4. At light at US-70, continue north on Pleasant Green Rd
    5. See "From US-70 and Pleasant Green", below
  • From Raleigh:
    1. West on I-40
    2. Exit 279B to North 147 (Durham Expressway)
    3. Stay on 147 to the end, merge onto I-85 South. Merge into one of the left two lanes.
    4. Exit 170 to US-70 (first exit). Stay in right-hand lane.
    5. Right on Pleasant Green Rd (first light)
    6. See "From US-70 and Pleasant Green", below
  • From (north) Durham:
    1. South on I-85
    2. Exit 170 to US-70. Stay in right-hand lane.
    3. Right on Pleasant Green Rd (first light)
    4. See "From US-70 and Pleasant Green", below
  • From US-70 and Pleasant Green:
    1. North on Pleasant Green Rd for about 5.8 miles
    2. At 4-way blinking stop light at St. Mary's Rd, continue north on Schley Rd for about 1.8 miles
    3. Right on New Sharon Church Rd. (at first stop sign -- Underwood's Gas/Grocery -- "pause marker" on map) for about 1 mile.
    4. Right on Terry Rd (first right), for about 0.1 miles
    5. Right on Woodland Park Dr (first right; dirt road), for about 0.7 miles
    6. Drive to end of Woodland Park, 4001 is on left.
  • From other areas (just hints, because you probably know your way):
    1. From south Durham, consider taking 147 to I-85, US-70, and then going north on Pleasant Green.
    2. From southwest Orange County, consider taking Orange Grove Rd into Hillsborough and then taking St Mary's Rd (perhaps via Lawrence Rd) to New Sharon Church Rd (which crosses Schley Rd at Underwood's Gas/Grocery).
    3. From north Orange County, consider taking Schley Rd south to New Sharon Church Rd. and going east to Terry.
    4. Note: The bridge on St. Mary's Road is OPEN for traffic again.
