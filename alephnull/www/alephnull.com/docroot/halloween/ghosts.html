Halloween 2004

Voyce-Faith-Axworthy Flying Ghosts

These ghosts are similar to (and inspired by) the traditional Axworthy Flying Ghosts. Key differences:

  • The posts are 15' 4x4s cut to fit into 4" PVC that is sunk 3' into the ground.
  • The posts are tied to trees behing them and tensioned using a turn buckle.
  • This tensioning allows the ghosts to weigh more than a pound (we've only tested with one ghost so far).




The drive mechanism is a geared motor mounted on one of the poles.







The ghosts contain circuitry for pulsating orange LED eyes, pulsating blue LEDs in the body, and a voice chip.
