Converse Logos

A logo is a design created for immediate recognition used by trademarks and commercial brands. Converse has developed through the years several different logos to which everyone, depending on how old you are, can be identified. It is the bond between the customer and the company. The 5 pointed star logo which can be identified as the Converse logo is a pentagram, some people says that it is a Freemason symbol and also an ancient occultic symbol.
